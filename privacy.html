Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who this covers

Personal Agent is a private assistant run by one operator for a small group of invited people. This policy explains what it accesses and how that data is used.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only), only if you connect it: message headers and contents needed for the goals you set, such as finding job-application confirmations or job notifications. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Chat messages you send it on Telegram or iMessage, and the replies it sends you.
  • What you tell it: goals, preferences and facts you share so it can help.

How it is used

  • Only to carry out the goals you set and to send you updates and suggestions.
  • Relevant parts may be sent to an AI model provider (currently OpenAI) to read and plan. They are sent only for that purpose.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else except as required by law.
  • People cannot see each other's data.

Google user data

Personal Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide features you asked for, is not used to train AI models, and is not read by humans except with your permission, for security, or as required by law.

Storage and deletion

  • Data is stored on the operator's own computer or private server.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time from your Google account permissions, or by asking the agent.
  • To have your data deleted, ask the agent or the operator; it will be removed.

Contact

Questions: contact the operator who invited you.
